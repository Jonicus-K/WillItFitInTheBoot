How To Know If Items Will Fit In Your Car Boot

Understand the 4 critical constraints: floor length, wheel arch pinch points, roof clearance, and rear window rake angle.

1. The Tailgate Aperture Gate

An item cannot travel inside your boot if it cannot pass through the rear opening. Tailgates are often narrower and shorter than the internal boot cabin. Many flat-pack boxes get stuck at the sill before ever reaching the boot floor.

2. Wheel Arch Width (The 100cm Pinch)

Most medium family hatchbacks feature between 95 cm and 105 cm of flat width between the interior rear wheel arches. Large items like 65" TV boxes (98 cm wide in packaging) require flat orientation or propping one edge up.

3. Sloping Hatch Glass (Rear Window Rake)

While an estate car or SUV offers a boxy rear tailgate, sleek hatchbacks and saloons feature steep rake angles (29° to 48°). Tall rectangular items can easily strike the rear windscreen if not angled forward or stowed with seats folded.

4. Seats Folded vs Seats Up Capacity

Folding rear seats typically doubles or triples usable cargo floor length (from ~75 cm to over 150-195 cm). Our 3D calculator models both modes with authentic angled seatback risers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will a 65-inch TV box fit in my car boot?

A standard boxed 65-inch TV measures approximately 160 cm long, 98 cm high, and 18 cm thick. In standard hatchbacks (like a VW Golf), it will not fit flat between the wheel arches without folding the rear seats. In estate cars (like the Ford Focus Estate or Skoda Octavia) and SUVs (like the Tesla Model Y), a 65-inch TV box fits flat when rear seats are folded.

Will a washing machine fit in a standard hatchback boot?

Standard UK/European domestic washing machines measure 60 cm wide, 60 cm deep, and 85 cm high. While the boot floor in most hatchbacks is wide enough (95–105 cm), the tailgate opening aperture height or boot roof height (often 67–75 cm) prevents upright transport. You will usually need to lay the machine flat or transport it in an estate or SUV with seats folded.

How does rear window rake angle affect cargo fitment?

Even if an item fits onto the flat floor length of your boot, a sloping rear window or raked hatchback tailgate reduces the usable length near the roof. Our dual-angle 3D solver checks both the floor dimensions and the upper rear hatch glass slope so your cargo doesn't contact or crack your rear glass.

Can an IKEA Billy bookcase fit in a car with seats folded?

A standard flat-packed IKEA Billy bookcase box is approximately 205 cm long, 30 cm wide, and 13 cm high. Because it exceeds 2 meters in length, it will typically require an estate car, an SUV with seats folded, or sliding the box slightly forward between the front bucket seats.
